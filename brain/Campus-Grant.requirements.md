# Campus-Grant.requirements

source: [[Campus Grant RFP.docx]] §3, §4

## requirements (§3)
- `R-1` SHALL report soil moisture at least every 15 minutes
- `R-2` SHALL run 30 days on battery without maintenance [superseded 2026-10-06 → [[Campus-Grant.amendment-1|amendment 1]]: 14 days]
- `R-3` SHALL encrypt data in transit
- `R-4` SHOULD provide an open data dashboard (SHOULD, not SHALL: scored, not required)

## evaluation (§4)
- technical approach 50, team experience 30, cost realism 20
- below 60 points: not funded (a floor, not a ranking)
