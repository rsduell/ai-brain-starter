Request for Proposals CSN-2026-01 (fictional example)

Section 1. Overview
The Example State Innovation Office invites proposals to deploy low cost environmental sensor networks on university farms. Total program funding is $250,000. Awards range from $25,000 to $75,000.

Section 2. Key dates
Questions due: 2026-11-02 17:00 CT.
Proposals due: 2026-11-16 17:00 CT.
Awards announced: 2026-12-14.

Section 3. Requirements
R-1. The network SHALL report soil moisture at least every 15 minutes.
R-2. The network SHALL operate for 30 days on battery without maintenance.
R-3. Data SHALL be encrypted in transit.
R-4. The proposer SHOULD provide an open data dashboard.

Section 4. Evaluation
Technical approach 50 points. Team experience 30 points. Cost realism 20 points. Proposals scoring below 60 are not funded.

Section 5. Submission
One PDF, 10 pages maximum, emailed to grants@example.org.
